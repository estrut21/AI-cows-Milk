Comparing AI and Comparing Cows.

AI and cows are not necessarily comparable, but they both create significant impacts in their areas. Artifical Intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science that has machines perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as learning and problem solving. Cows, on the other hand, are domesticated animals that are raised for their milk and meat. While AI is a rapidly advancing technology that has the potential to advance many industries, cows have been an important part of human agriculture for thousands of years. Comparing AI and cows is not as easy as it sounds, because they are very different. However, both AI and cows have their own unique aspects to society.


Problems with AI

There are actually many challenges with AI such as misinformation, bias, and job displacement. Misinformation is a major concern with AI, as it can be used to creat chaos and fake news. Job displacement is also another concern with AI, because of the potential for AI to replace human workers in entry level positions. Additionally, AI relies on data collection. This leads to mass data leaks and privacy concerns. Then the most comparable to cows is the enviornmental impact of AI. They use massive cooling data centers and that interacting with AI chatbots consume around 16 ounces of water every 10 to 50 messages. Another issue is AI resources mines AI es massive electricity consumption, heavy freshwater usage for data centers, and contributes to carbon emissions. The carbon emissions contribute to global warming. The more people that use AI makes the demand for the enegy increase, which makes its impact on the enviorment an important issue to consider.


Comparison

According to Bryant Research based in the UK, AI (like ChatGPT, claude, etc) does use a lot of water, but cows and farming where they highlight dairy and beef uses massively more, so if you truly want to save water then the farming is the bigger issue to focus on. So to repeat while AI does consume billions of liters of water, its impact is still much smaller than the water used for farming. The author Abby Couture makes it a point to say thay AIs water consumption shouldnt be ignored but efforts to perserve water should be focused on farming.
