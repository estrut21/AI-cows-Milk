The Cow Problem.

Cows and Agriculture are the largest source of methane emissions. Methane is a potent greenhouse gas that contributes to climate change. Additionally, dairy farming requries large amounts of water and land, leading to deforestation and habitat loss. By choosing AI Milk, you can help reduce your carbon footprint and support a more sustainable future.


According to the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), the average cow emits between 154 to 264 pounds of methane gas per year By reducing the demand for dairy products, we can help decrease the number of cows needed for milk production, which in turn reduces methane emissions and helps combat climate change.
